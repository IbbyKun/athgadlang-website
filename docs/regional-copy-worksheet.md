# Regional service copy: what your advisers need to fill in

For: *"In the [Bahrain / UK / PK / KSA] region, wherever UAE is mentioned, it
should be changed to [region]… if the same tax applies."*

The site can now serve different service copy per region. This is the list of
what needs writing, so whoever advises in each region can answer a page at a
time instead of reading the whole site.

Fill in the blanks, send it back, and we drop it in. A blank left blank means
that region keeps the UAE wording.

---

## First, the trap

A find-and-replace on "UAE" does not do this job, and would leave the pages
looking localised while still being wrong.

There are **32 lines** containing the literal word "UAE". But the UAE-specific
claims that never use the word are just as many:

| Term | Uses | Why it is UAE-specific |
|---|---|---|
| Mainland / Free Zone | 19 | UAE licensing jurisdictions. Bahrain, the UK and Pakistan do not have this structure. |
| FTA | 7 | The UAE Federal Tax Authority. Saudi has ZATCA, the UK has HMRC, Pakistan has the FBR. |
| Golden Visa | 5 | A UAE residency programme. No equivalent in the other four. |
| PRO Services | 2 | Exists because of how UAE government departments require a registered liaison. |
| WPS | 1 | The UAE Wage Protection System. |
| ESR | 1 | UAE Economic Substance Regulations. |

Change "UAE" to "UK" and the page still tells a British reader about Free Zone
ownership, FTA audits and the Golden Visa.

## 2. Corporate Services — probably not a per-region rewrite

`/services/corporate-services`. Thirty-one region-specific references, and
unlike Tax this is not a page that can be localised sentence by sentence,
because the *service itself* is UAE-shaped: company formation across Mainland /
Free Zone / Offshore, trade licences, PRO services, Golden Visa, UAE bank
account opening, trademark registration "across the UAE and GCC".

**So the question is not "what is the Bahraini wording", it is:**

they cannot buy. Tell us which regions, and we will hide it there.

## How to send it back

Any format. This table filled in, a document, or an email per region — whatever
is quickest for the people writing it. What matters is that each replacement is
attributable to someone who advises in that jurisdiction, because these are
statements about tax law published under the firm's name.
